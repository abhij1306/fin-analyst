# Decisions: D2 (PRs #69, #70, #71)

Product-level decisions only (what affects the analysis or what the user sees). Builder readings, not owner decisions.

## Ledger Vouchers need their period's trial balance first (E811)

- **Decision:** Ingesting Ledger Vouchers before the period's trial balance is refused.
- **Why:** The trial balance says which ledgers are parties, and Tally leaves zero-balance ledgers out of it. Without it fin cannot mask party names in the vouchers.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/sources.py: ingest_source` (drop the check and mask in a later pass).

## A Ledger Vouchers ledger not in its trial balance is masked as LEDG-0001

- **Decision:** Such a ledger gets a `LEDG-` token, and a name first masked as LEDG keeps that token if a later file finds it under a party group.
- **Why:** Fin cannot tell whether it is a party; over-masking is safer than leaking a name. One ledger must not have two tokens across years.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/sources.py: _load_lv` (store the name as filed for non-parties); `diligence/mask.py: Masker.token`.

## A row that cannot be read is refused, never dropped

- **Decision:** An undated row with amounts that the profile does not skip is E204; a Ledger Vouchers file with no voucher line is E204.
- **Why:** Silently dropping lines would change figures.
- **How to change it:** Add the row's name to `[rows] skip` in the profile.

## Group sum differences are findings; a group-only trial balance is refused

- **Decision:** A group not equal to its ledgers is a finding (`tb_group_sum`), rows kept as filed. A trial balance with no ledger rows is E809.
- **Why:** What a company filed that does not add up is a finding, not a failure. Group rows alone cannot map to accounts.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/tally.py: group_sum_findings, trial_balance_rows`.

## The period must be one full year; a later file supersedes

- **Decision:** The export period must be a year of the engagement (E808), or exactly its stub. A later file of the same kind and period supersedes the earlier one and its findings; the same sha256 is always E105.
- **Why:** Matches how documents behave: a re-export replaces the earlier one.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/sources.py: _supersede`.

## Names are never printed or queried

- **Decision:** `fin sql` naming `mask_values`, or calling `query`, `query_table`, `read_duckdb`, is E810; writes to source tables are E806.
- **Why:** Guards against reading names or undoing masking by accident. It is not a sandbox.
- **How to change it:** `query/run.py: refuse_masked`; `query/write.py: DILIGENCE_TABLE`.

## G1's trial balance checks apply only once an engagement has a trial balance, Ledger Vouchers or stub (builder reading)

- **Decision:** With none of them, G1 keeps its D1 checklist and shows one passing line saying the checks are not active.
- **Why:** The plan says D2 adds a trial balance and Ledger Vouchers per period; making them mandatory would break every D1 engagement that approves G1 on statements alone.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/tb_checks.py: checklist` (remove the early return).

## Current-year profit from income and expense ledgers counts into other equity in tb_vs_statements (builder reading)

- **Decision:** P&L ledgers also roll into `other_equity`.
- **Why:** Tally lists the year's income and expenses beside the opening Profit & Loss A/c, while the balance sheet's other equity includes the year's profit. Without it every year raises a false query on other equity.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/recon.py: RETAINED, _roll_up`.

## Only confirmed ledger mappings count

- **Decision:** Only confirmed ledger mappings count in reconciliations and the stub. Fin never confirms one.
- **Why:** A mapping is the user's call; an unconfirmed one could silently move a figure.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/ledgers.py: read`.

## Reconciliation queries

- **Decision:** Differences at or above the engagement threshold get a neutral draft query with the two amounts. No query while any ledger is unmapped. A re-run reuses a query only when the account and both amounts match and it is not withdrawn. The stub cannot be reconciled (E812).
- **Why:** The difference may be fin's own mapping gap; the stub has no audited statement.
- **How to change it:** `diligence/recon.py: _reconcile, _earlier_query`.
